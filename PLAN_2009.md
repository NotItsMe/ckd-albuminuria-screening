# Pre-specified plan: one-time check on NHANES 2009–2010 (written 2026-11-08, before any scoring)
- Model: frozen final model, model_main_racefree.txt (race-free LightGBM, 18 inputs), refit on nothing, no recalibration.
- Population: adults 18+, examined, with urine albumin, urine creatinine, serum creatinine, HbA1c; no diabetes (self-report or HbA1c >= 6.5%); CKD-EPI 2021 eGFR >= 60. Same definitions as training (BP = mean of first 3 readings; pulse = first reading).
- Outcome (main): UACR >= 30 mg/g on the first (exam) urine sample.
- Main measure: share of albuminuria results found when the model tests the same weighted share as the high blood pressure rule, vs the rule; gain with Rao-Wu bootstrap 95% CI. Secondary: AUC; Bragg-Gresham Model 1 at the same volume.
- Exploratory: among participants with a second (home first-morning) sample, results high on both samples (persistent) found by model vs rule, with case count reported.
- The result is reported whatever it shows. This cycle was not used in any development step.
